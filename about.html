About us

Securing your assets, aligned with your values.

Yakin Hijrah Ventures is a provider of professional business development and training solutions, and a takaful intermediary serving businesses across Malaysia.

Who we are

Established in 2024, we specialise in comprehensive takaful solutions and training programmes designed to equip sales intermediaries, individuals and organisations with the skills needed to excel in a competitive market.

With a team of experienced industry professionals and subject matter experts, we deliver customised takaful solutions and training that meet the particular needs of businesses across many sectors — rather than pushing a fixed catalogue at everyone who walks through the door.

Our commitment is to ethical, Shariah-compliant financial solutions shaped around the real demands of our clients. With YHV, you are not only securing your assets — you are aligning your financial protection with your values.
