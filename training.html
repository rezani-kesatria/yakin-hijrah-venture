Training & development

Your team deserves the training the big companies give theirs.

Tailored development programmes for businesses, sales organisations and SMEs — built and delivered by people who ran these functions at Malaysia’s largest insurers, and structured so that a company of any size can actually say yes.

Why we exist

You shouldn’t need an HR department to develop your people properly.

Good training has quietly become a large-company benefit. Programmes are built around enterprise budgets, minimum headcounts and fixed public calendars — none of which suit a business running on fifteen people and a full order book.

We structure our programmes the other way round: sized to the team you actually have, delivered in the format that fits your week, and quoted per programme so you know what you’re committing to before you commit.

Sized to your team

Not a public course you send two people to and hope something sticks. The programme is built for the group in the room.

Delivered how it suits you

Online, on-site or hybrid — arranged around your operating hours rather than a fixed training calendar.

Quoted before you commit

A written proposal for your specific programme, at no cost and with no obligation to proceed.

Our speciality

Reactivating the sales people everyone else has written off.

Most agencies recruit their way out of a performance problem. It is almost always cheaper — and considerably faster — to restart the people already on your books.

Our Sales Reactivation programme is built specifically for organisations carrying non-performing or dormant sales teams: diagnosing why production stopped, rebuilding confidence and habits, and putting a measurable structure around the return.

Delivery formats

Three ways to run it

Every programme below can be delivered in any of these formats. The right one usually depends on how far apart your people sit, and how much of the week you can afford to give up.

Online

Live virtual sessions for teams spread across branches or regions. The lowest overhead option — no travel, no venue, no downtime getting there.

On-site

We come to your premises. Best where the work is hands-on, where the group needs to be in one room, or where the material is specific to your operation.

Hybrid

Intensive sessions on-site, follow-through delivered online. Keeps momentum without repeatedly pulling the whole team off the floor.
